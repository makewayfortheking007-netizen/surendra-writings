Aakhir Tu Darta Hai Kyun?

05 · 06 · 2024
Aakhir tu darta hai kyun ? Log kya soche usse farak padta hai kyun! Haar gaya tu race apni toh khudse itni nafrat krta hai kyun Ye bhaag daud antim nhi , tera harna hamesha Tay nhi Toh fir aakhir darta hai kyun ?
Sapne toh khoob sanjhoye tune, khwaab deke hai duniya Jahan ke tune. Toh duniya ke inn taano se Mann vichlit hota hai kyun ? Aakhir Harne se itna darta hai kyun ?
Keech de apni iss train ki chain ko tu, Chood iss unchahe Safar ko tu,badh chal apne sapno ki aur tu paale apna chayan(peace) firse . Safar ye naya hai mukaam ye unsunna h, Dil mei bhar ke sapno ki saansein chal pad tu , Duniya ki kahaniyon se kyun sehemta hai tu Aakhir tu darta hai kyun?
Chal pad apne raaste par, tu gir padega tu lad padega tu jhuk padega Par kabhi na marr sakega tera ye sapna. Aakhir Mann maar kar jeeta hai tu kyun?
— Surendra Kumar
