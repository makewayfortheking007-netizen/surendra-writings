Film Script
Sequence: "Inside the house"

SCENES:

1. INT. HOUSE - DAY

SOUND of a quiet, rhythmic ticking clock throughout.

The scene opens with a man with his head down and sleeping on the table. He suddenly wakes up and hears the chirping of birds.

Fast shots of him opening the tap (water gushing sound), brushing his teeth (scraping of teeth sound), and wiping his mouth. Followed by the shot of him going down to the kitchen and pouring coffee into his cup, and sipping it slowly.

CLOSE UP (CU) - HANDS:

The Man's pale fingers meticulously arrange three identical steel spoons, perfectly parallel, next to an empty plate, which sits on the floor. He pours hot water into a cup. The movements are slow and precise.

MID SHOT (MS) - Floor:

The Man sits alone in the large dining hall, white marble floor. He eats plain rice from the bowl. The room is vast, high-ceilinged (over the head shot from 2nd floor), and almost sparsely furnished. The ticking clock is slightly louder here.

WIDE SHOT (WS) - LIVING ROOM:

The Man performs a single, slow push-up in the exact centre of the empty living room. He holds the "up" position for an unnaturally long time, perfectly still, before slowly lowering himself.

CUT TO

POINT OF VIEW (POV) - WINDOW:

From his perspective, looking out a clean window. We see only a quiet street outside: a single car drives by, then silence.

CU - FINGERS:

His index finger slowly traces the floral pattern on the bed. His movement is devoid of curiosity.

MID SHOT (MS) - Reflection of window:

Shows his reflection captured from the window (5-10 sec shot). He is sitting straight on a chair and starts a song on his phone. It's an old song from Bollywood. The camera focuses on him putting his head down, and the song plays slowly in the background.

Next shot of him waking up suddenly, same as the one in the beginning.

This whole sequence repeats two times, to show the passing of time.

[The montage always concludes with:

INT. HOUSE - NIGHT He is sitting on the chair and puts his head down, listening to the same song.]

2. INT. HOUSE - NIGHT

The Man sits on a long sofa. The room is dimly lit by a single, small light, casting long, unsettling shadows. He stares blankly at an unpowered, dark television screen. The rhythmic TICKING CLOCK is the only sound.

SOUND of a distant, metallic GATE CREAKING open.

The Man's head snaps up, his body tenses. His eyes, in the low light, are wide and alert, not with curiosity, but with faint fear.

The camera PANS SLOWLY, now a tight close-up (CU). He is looking directly into the camera lens, not blinking. His breathing is shallow, almost non-existent.

SOUND of car doors shutting, muffled voices speaking softly.

He breaks his stare. His fear transforms, and he quietly climbs up the stairs.

3. INT. HOUSE CONTINUOUS

ACTION MODE: The camera, now moving smoothly, follows THE MAN as he runs silently, almost gliding, up the stairs. Each step is perfectly weighted, making no sound. He hides inside a dark room just beside the entrance. His body is pressed against the wall, and he is breathing slowly. He stares into the darkness and remains still.

4. EXT. FRONT DOOR - CONTINUOUS

A shot of THE MOTHER and her DAUGHTER standing at the front door. The Mother is fumbling with a key, her movements slightly hesitant.

The lock CLICKS. The Mother pushes the heavy door open, revealing the dimly lit interior.

5. INT. HALLWAY - CONTINUOUS

The Mother and Daughter step casually into the hall, tired from their trip to the little girl's grandparents.

The mother notices a small light is on, and waves off, thinking she might have left it on. On the kitchen counter, visible from the hall, sits the bowl with the perfectly arranged spoons inside.

The mother enters and is confused, and fear starts to settle in.

“What is this?” she thinks to herself.

The Daughter clings to her mother's arm, her face pale with fear.

MOTHER (signals the Daughter to stay quiet, and that she is going upstairs to check)

The Mother gives her daughter a quick, reassuring squeeze. She walks slowly, deliberately, towards 1st floor.

INT. 1st Floor - CONTINUOUS

LOW ANGLE Close Up - THE MAN: He is now standing in the darkest corner of the room, almost invisible. His breathing is shallow. His hand moves, slowly, to his waistband. He extracts a long, gleaming kitchen knife. A dim light reflects off the blade, revealing the sharpness of the blade. There is no fear in his eyes now, only a chilling, predatory stillness. He hears footsteps closing in.

INT. Room ENTRANCE - CONTINUOUS

The Mother reaches the room's doorway. Her hand finds the main light switch on the wall. She hesitates for a beat, gathering her courage.

CU - MOTHER'S FINGERS:

Her fingers, trembling slightly, reach for and flip the switch.

SOUND of a sharp, loud CLICK.

6. INT. Room - CONTINUOUS

The harsh, cold white light FLICKERS on, then hums to full brightness, illuminating the room.

The Man is no longer in the corner. He stands silently 2 feet from the mother, the knife held loosely at his side.

The Mother freezes, her eyes widening in disbelief and then utter terror.

The man SMILES for a brief period. It's the smile of a demon.

[His face looks terrifying, an empty expression, devoid of warmth. Their eyes lock.]

A RAPID, BACK-AND-FORTH SEQUENCE OF EXTREME CLOSE UPS:

1. EXTREME CU - THE MOTHER'S EYES: Wide open, filled with pure horror. A single, panicked gasp escapes her.

2. EXTREME CU - THE MAN'S EYES: Cold, still, utterly emotionless.

3. EXTREME CU - THE MOTHER'S EYES: Her mouth opens slightly, a silent scream forming, but is cut short...

4. EXTREME CU - THE MAN: He lunges forward and slashes the throat.

CUT TO BLACK.

SOUND of a wet, sharp SLASH.

There is now an eerie silence inside the house.

OVER THE HEAD SHOT - Girl standing alone in the hall, unaware of what has happened upstairs.

7. EXT. STREET - NIGHT

SOUND of a high-pitched, aggressive POLICE SIREN growing rapidly louder.

A POLICE VEHICLE, its red and blue lights speeding down the street at full speed.

8. EXT. Random Location - Night (The next day)

The scene opens with a shot of a silent, moonless night.

The camera pans to the front door of a house where 3-4 old newspapers lie.

Close UP - A hand comes into the frame and picks up the paper. camera focuses on the headline, it reads....

"MOTHER AND DAUGHTER FOUND BRUTALLY MURDERED IN HOME. KILLER AT LARGE."

The man enters the house.

We follow THE MAN from behind. He walks calmly through the spacious, empty hall of a new, different house. The house is empty and sparsely furnished. He moves with a familiar rhythm.

We take a close-up of him biting into an apple, as soon as he bites, and we hear a crunch, end scene.

FADE TO BLACK

The title ROZMARRA fades in slowly.

THE END

About the film

Rozmarra

"Rozmarra" is a story of a homeless loner who secretly lives in empty homes and moves from one to another, until his routine is shattered when the real owners of one house return unexpectedly.

You never hear any dialogue in this film; it is dictated by visuals and the sound of the actions. And the face of the man is never revealed, only the close-up of his eyes, mouth, hands, and silhouette.
